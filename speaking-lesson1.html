IELTS Speaking Part 2: Cue Card Lesson

In Part 2 of the IELTS Speaking test, you will get a card with a topic. You have 1 minute to prepare notes and must talk for 1 to 2 minutes.

📋 Topic Card: Describe a beautiful place you visited

You should say:

  • Where this place is
  • When you went there
  • What you did there
  • And explain why you think it is beautiful.

🌟 Band 7.5+ Model Answer

"Today, I’d like to talk about a breathtaking place I visited last summer called Bagan, located in upper Myanmar. It is an ancient city renowned for its thousands of majestic Buddhist temples and pagodas spread across a vast green plain."

"I traveled there with my family for a short vacation. During our stay, we rented e-bikes to explore the archaeological zone. The most memorable experience was watching the sunrise from a viewing mound. As the sun crept up, the morning mist vanished, and the silhouettes of countless pagodas emerged against the golden sky. It was an absolute feast for the eyes."

"I find Bagan exceptionally beautiful because of its peaceful atmosphere and historical grandeur. It feels like stepping back in time, and the architectural wonders left me completely spellbound."
