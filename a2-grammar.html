A2 Essential Grammar

Learn the key grammar rules you need for daily communication at the A2 (Elementary) level. (A2 အဆင့်အတွက် အဓိကကျတဲ့ သဒ္ဒါစည်းမျဉ်းများ)

1. Present Simple vs Present Continuous

Present Simple — for routines, facts and habits. (ပုံမှန်လုပ်နေကျအရာများ) e.g. "I work every day."

Present Continuous — for actions happening now. (အခုဖြစ်ပျက်နေသောအရာများ) e.g. "I am working now."

2. Past Simple (Regular & Irregular)

Regular verbs: add -ed → "play" → "played"

Irregular verbs: change → "go" → "went", "eat" → "ate"

✅ "Yesterday, I played football and I went to the market."

3. Future: "Going to" vs "Will"

"Going to" — for plans already made. (ကြိုတင်စီစဉ်ထားသောအရာ) e.g. "I am going to visit my friend tomorrow."

"Will" — for quick decisions and predictions. (လွယ်ကူသောဆုံးဖြတ်ချက်နှင့် ခန့်မှန်းချက်) e.g. "I think it will rain."

4. Modal Verbs (Can, Could, Would)

Can = ability (စွမ်းရည်) — "I can swim."

Could = polite request / past ability — "Could you help me?"

Would = polite offers — "Would you like some tea?"
