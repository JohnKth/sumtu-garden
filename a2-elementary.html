A2 Elementary English Course

⏱️ Duration: 4 Months 📊 Level: Elementary 🎓 Certificate: Yes

Build a solid foundation in English. This course is designed to boost your confidence in daily communication, grammar, vocabulary, and active speaking skills.

Part 2: Past Experiences & Storytelling

Describe past events, holidays, and personal life histories.
Grammar: Past Simple with Regular & Irregular verbs (အတိတ်ကာလ သဒ္ဒါအသုံးအနှုန်းများ)

Part 3: Practical Communication

Making requests, ordering food in restaurants, and asking for directions.
Grammar: Modal verbs (Can, Could, Would) and Prepositions of place (အကူကြိယာများနှင့် နေရာပြဝိဘတ်များ)

Part 4: Future Plans & Ambitions

Making predictions, talking about future arrangements, and travel plans.
Grammar: Future with 'Going to' and 'Will' (အနာဂတ်ကာလ ပြသခြင်း)
