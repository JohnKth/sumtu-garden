2. Conversational Chunks

Asking about the weather (ရာသီဥတု မေးမြန်းခြင်း):

  • 🗣️ "What's the weather like today?" (ဒီနေ့ ရာသီဥတု ဘယ်လိုရှိလဲ။)
  • 🗣️ "What's the weather like in Thailand?" (ထိုင်းနိုင်ငံမှာ ရာသီဥတု ဘယ်လိုရှိလဲ။)
  • 🗣️ "What is your favorite type of weather?" (ဒီနေ့ ရာသီဥတု ဘယ်လိုရှိလဲ။)
  • 🗣️ "Do you like rainy days? Why?" (ထိုင်းနိုင်ငံမှာ ရာသီဥတု ဘယ်လိုရှိလဲ။)
  • 🗣️ "What do you wear when it is cold?" (ဒီနေ့ ရာသီဥတု ဘယ်လိုရှိလဲ။)
  • 🗣️ "What do you do when it is sunny?" (ထိုင်းနိုင်ငံမှာ ရာသီဥတု ဘယ်လိုရှိလဲ။)
  • 🗣️ "What is the weather usually like in your country?" (ဒီနေ့ ရာသီဥတု ဘယ်လိုရှိလဲ။)
  • 🗣️ "Which do you prefer: hot weather or cold weather?" (ထိုင်းနိုင်ငံမှာ ရာသီဥတု ဘယ်လိုရှိလဲ။)
  • 🗣️ "What can you do on a rainy day?" (ဒီနေ့ ရာသီဥတု ဘယ်လိုရှိလဲ။)

Answering (ပြန်လည်ဖြေကြားခြင်း):

  • 💬 "It is + [adjective]." (ဥပမာ - It is sunny and hot. / It is rainy and cold.)
Example Dialogue (A2 Level Small Talk):
Teacher: "Hi Thura! What's the weather like outside today?"
Student: "Hi Teacher! It is very cloudy and cool today. I think it will rain soon!"

3. Grammar Focus

Noun vs Adjective Form (နာမ်နှင့် နာမဝိသေသန ကွာခြားပုံ):

ရာသီဥတုကို ဖော်ပြတဲ့အခါ "It is + Adjective" ပုံစံကို သုံးရပါမယ်။ နာမ် (Noun) စကားလုံးတွေရဲ့ အနောက်မှာ "-y" ထည့်ပေးခြင်းဖြင့် Adjective ပြောင်းလဲနိုင်ပါတယ် [for example, 1.2.5]။

• Rain (မိုး - Noun) ➡️ It is rainy (မိုးရွာနေတယ် - Adjective)
• Sun (နေ - Noun) ➡️ It is sunny (နေသာနေတယ် - Adjective)
• Wind (လေ - Noun) ➡️ It is windy (လေတိုက်နေတယ် - Adjective)

4. CEFR A2 Exam Practice

CEFR A2 Key • Part 1 Multiple Choice

Read and choose the correct answer:

1. Sarah is wearing her coat, scarf, and gloves because it is ________ outside today.

A) sunny and hot
B) snowy and cold
C) dry and warm

2. We cannot see the buildings clearly because it is very ________ this morning.

A) foggy
B) sunny
C) windy

3. It is very hot and the sun is shining. The weather is ________.

A) snowy
B) foggy
C) sunny

4. Take an umbrella. It is ________.

A) rainy
B) windy
C) snowy

5. The trees are moving because there is a lot of ________.

A) fog
B) wind
C) snow

6. I can't see the road clearly because it is ________.

A) foggy
B) sunny
C) hot

7. We can make a snowman when the weather is ________.

A) rainy
B) snowy
C) humid

8. There are many dark clouds and there is thunder. It is ________.

A) stormy
B) sunny
C) dry

9. Look at the trees! It is very ________ today, so it is a good day to fly a kite.

A) cloudy
B) rainy
C) windy

10. Take your umbrella with you because it is ________ heavily outside.

A) sunning
B) raining
C) blowing
