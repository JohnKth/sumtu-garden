🎒 Clothes & Accessories

📖 Word List & Definitions

Backpack noun/ˈbæk.pæk/
📖 a bag that you carry on your back
🇲🇲 ကျောပိုးအိတ်
💡 "He packed his textbooks into his backpack before school."
Raincoat noun/ˈreɪn.koʊt/
📖 a coat that keeps you dry when it rains
🇲🇲 မိုးကာအင်္ကျီ
💡 "Take a raincoat with you; it looks like rain."
Trainers noun/ˈreɪ.nəz/
📖 comfortable shoes that you wear for running or sports
🇲🇲 အားကစားဖိနပ်
💡 "I always wear comfortable trainers when I go running."
Trousers noun/ˈtraʊ.zəz/
📖 a piece of clothing that covers your legs
🇲🇲 ဘောင်းဘီရှည်
💡 "He wore smart black trousers to the job interview."
