📈 2. Core Vocabulary Upgrades

Useful Technology (နည်းပညာက အသုံးဝင်တယ်) ကို အဆင့်မြှင့်ပြောနည်း:

• Band 6: useful technology
• Band 7: useful tool
• Band 8: a valuable tool
• Band 9: a powerful and versatile tool (အသုံးပြုနိုင်တဲ့ နည်းလမ်းမျိုးစုံရှိသော)

"Technology has become a powerful and versatile tool for communication and education."

🗣️ 3. Real Part 3 Questions & Cognitive Frameworks

Q1. Has technology made life better?

⚙️ Strategy: BENEFIT ➡️ LIMITATION ➡️ BALANCE

Band 9 Model Answer:
"Overall, I'd argue that technology has improved quality of life, but the picture is far from straightforward. On the one hand, it has dramatically increased convenience, connectivity and access to information. On the other hand, our growing dependence on digital technology can affect attention, privacy and even the quality of face-to-face relationships. So, I'd say technology itself isn't inherently positive or negative; its impact largely depends on how consciously we use it."

Q2. Will AI (Artificial Intelligence) replace human workers?

⚙️ Strategy: AUTOMATION ➡️ EFFICIENCY ➡️ JOB DISPLACEMENT ➡️ ADAPTATION

Band 9 Model Answer:
"I don't think AI will simply replace human workers across the board. It's much more likely to automate certain routine tasks while changing the nature of many existing jobs. Some occupations may certainly face disruption, particularly those involving repetitive and predictable tasks. At the same time, new roles are likely to emerge... So, the bigger challenge is ensuring that workers have opportunities to retrain and adapt to a changing labour market."

Q3. Is online learning better than classroom learning?

⚙️ Strategy: NO ABSOLUTE STATEMENT • THE BLENDED APPROACH

Band 9 Model Answer:
"I wouldn't say one is universally better than the other. Online learning offers remarkable flexibility, particularly for people who have work or family commitments... However, traditional classroom learning has advantages that are difficult to replicate online, particularly when it comes to spontaneous interaction... Personally, I think a blended approach is probably the most effective."

💰 4. Band 9 Technology Chunk Bank

🟢 Positive Impact Chunks:
• increase convenience
• improve efficiency
• enhance productivity
• expand access to information
• widen access to education
🔴 Negative/Limitation Chunks:
• become overly reliant on
• lose touch with real-world interaction
• suffer from information overload
• struggle to concentrate without screens
• widen the digital divide

🔑 5. Quick Exam Day Tips

• Start with a clear stance before giving reasons (e.g., "Overall, I'd argue that...").

• Use the BENEFIT ➡️ LIMITATION ➡️ BALANCE structure to sound balanced.

• Drop in at least two Band 9 chunks from the bank above.

• Always finish with a perspective line: "It really depends on how we choose to use it."
