Lesson 1.3: Topic Pack (Hometown & Job)

Part 1 တွင် အမေးအများဆုံး မကွဲမဖြစ်ခေါင်းစဉ်ကြီး ၂ ခု၏ မေးခွန်းနှင့် အဖြေလွှာများ

🏡 Topic A: Hometown

❓ Q1: Where is your hometown?
"Well, I come from Yangon, which is the commercial hub of Myanmar. It is a bustling metropolis known for its unique blend of colonial architecture and modern skyscrapers, and it houses the famous Shwedagon Pagoda."
💡 Vocabulary
  • Commercial hub: စီးပွားရေးဗဟိုချက်မ
  • Metropolis: မြို့ကြီးပြကြီး
🔥 High-Scoring Chunks
  • "Unique blend of..." (ထူးခြားတဲ့ပေါင်းစပ်မှု)
  • "It houses the famous..." (...တည်ရှိရာနေရာဖြစ်သည်)
❓ Q2: What do you like most about your hometown?
"What I adore most about my city is its vibrant culinary scene. You can find an incredible variety of mouth-watering street food at almost any hour of the day. On top of that, the local people are exceptionally hospitable and welcoming."
💡 Vocabulary
  • Culinary scene: အစားအသောက်ယဉ်ကျေးမှုကဏ္ဍ
  • Hospitable: ဧည့်ဝတ်ကျေပွန်သော
🔥 High-Scoring Chunks
  • "What I adore most about... is..." (ကျွန်တော်အနှစ်သက်ဆုံးအရာကတော့...)
  • "On top of that..." (ဒါ့အပြင်...)

💼 Topic B: Work or Job

❓ Q3: Do you work or are you a student?
"Currently, I am gainfully employed as a full-time English instructor at an academy. I’ve been running this role for roughly two years now, and I find it both challenging and immensely rewarding."
💡 Vocabulary
  • Gainfully employed: အလုပ်အကိုင်အတည်တကျရှိသော
  • Immensely rewarding: အလွန်အကျိုးရှိ/ပီတိဖြစ်ရသော
🔥 High-Scoring Chunks
  • "For roughly [time] now..." (အကြမ်းဖျင်း...ရှိပြီဖြစ်သည်)
  • "I find it both... and..." (၎င်းက...ရော...ပါဖြစ်တယ်လို့ခံစားရတယ်)
❓ Q4: What are your main responsibilities at work?
"My primary duties involve designing comprehensive lesson plans and delivering lectures to adult students. Additionally, I am responsible for evaluating student performance to ensure they are on track to achieve their targeted band scores."
💡 Vocabulary
  • Primary duties: အဓိကတာဝန်ဝတ္တရားများ
  • Evaluating: အကဲဖြတ်ဆန်းစစ်ခြင်း
🔥 High-Scoring Chunks
  • "My primary duties involve [verb-ing]..." (ကျွန်တော့်အဓိကအလုပ်က...)
  • "Ensure they are on track to..." (...လမ်းကြောင်းမှန်ပေါ်ရှိနေစေရန်သေချာစေသည်)
